Section 17 — Important Arrival & Access Information
⚠ Critical Safety Instruction
To Note Before You Leave Philippolis:
Before you leave Philippolis to head to Kishindo / Tiger Canyon, please call the Canyon Lodge Manager on +27 66 290 8424. The reason for this is along the way you will lose cell reception. She will arrange someone to meet you at the main gate.
Check-in and Check-out Times

• Check-in time: Strictly 14h00

• Check-out time: Strictly 10h00

Due to the remote wilderness logistics and game drive schedules, early check-ins and late check-outs cannot be accommodated.

Driving Directions
Route Distance & Directions
1) From Bloemfontein 120 km on the N1 — take R717 at Trompsburg, 60 km to Philippolis. Once in Philippolis, follow the reserve signposts.
2) From Cape Town Take R717 at Colesberg — 60 km to Philippolis. Once in Philippolis, follow the reserve signposts.
