# Kishindo Conservation Reserve — Lodge Anchor User Guide

**Portal URL:** `https://kishindo-guests.pages.dev/admin` (or `/administrator`)  
**Access Level:** `Anchor Role` (Daily Operations, Guest Experience & Logistics)

---

## 1. Overview of the Anchor Role
The **Anchor Role** is designed for Lodge Anchors, Front of House Managers, and Hospitality Coordinators stationed at Kishindo Canyon Lodge. Your daily priorities include:
- **Welcoming Guests:** Tracking upcoming arrivals, prompt 14h00 check-in logistics, and suite allocations.
- **Curating Stays:** Reviewing guest pre-arrival forms (dietary requirements, allergies, medical notes, celebration milestones).
- **Team Coordination:** Providing immediate, copy-ready briefs to the Executive Chef, lead trackers, and photographic safari guides.
- **Dispatching Guest Communications:** Sending personalized pre-arrival welcome emails and PIN reminders.

---

## 2. Signing In to the Operations Portal
1. Open your browser and go to: **`https://kishindo-guests.pages.dev/admin`**
2. Enter your Kishindo staff email address and password.
3. Click **Access Lodge Operations →**.
4. In the top-right header, confirm your role pill displays:  
   `[Your Name] · Anchor Role` (highlighted with a soft blue badge).

> [!TIP]
> Need to check what a guest sees or help a guest log in? Click **`🔑 Guest Login Portal ↗`** in the dashboard top bar.

---

## 3. The Lodge Operations Dashboard
The main dashboard displays all active reservations categorized by their operational status:

| Stage Badge | Meaning & Operational Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`Provisional`** | Inquiry or provisional hold. Awaiting final confirmation or deposit. |
| **`Confirmed`** | Booking secured. Pre-arrival email invitation sent or ready to send. |
| **`Sanctuary Ready`** | Guest has completed their pre-arrival form! Kitchen, housekeeping, and guiding briefs are ready. |
| **`Cancelled`** | Reservation cancelled. Kept for historical audit. |

Click on any reservation card to open its **Booking Detail View**.

---

## 4. Reviewing Guest Pre-Arrival Responses

When a guest submits their pre-arrival form via their suite PIN, their responses update in real time on your dashboard.

### Key Sections to Review:
1. **Dietary & Allergies (Kitchen Briefing):**
   - Specific diets: Vegetarian, Vegan, Halaal, Gluten-Free, Nut Allergies, Dairy-Free.
   - Any custom medical allergies (e.g. bee venom, asthma).
2. **Room & Bed Configuration (Housekeeping):**
   - Confirm whether the suite setup is `King Bed` or `Two Twin Single Beds`.
3. **Arrival & Transfer Logistics:**
   - Transport method: Airlink via Bloemfontein, private charter, or self-drive.
   - Flight numbers and estimated arrival times (Prompt 14h00 required).
4. **Safari & Photographic Focus:**
   - Specific interests: Tiger photography, Cheetah metapopulation tracking, night drives, starlit dining.
5. **Special Celebrations:**
   - Anniversaries, birthdays, milestone celebrations for kitchen and lodge team surprises.

## 5. Operations 1-Click Copy & Paste (Briefing the Team)

Inside every booking detail view is the **Operations Copy & Paste** tool.

1. Click **📋 Copy Summary**.
2. Paste directly into WhatsApp groups or email threads for:
   - **Executive Chef:** Instant dietary and allergy checklist.
   - **Lead Guide & Trackers:** Guest photographic experience levels and requested focus.
   - **Host & Anchors:** Arrival times, emergency contacts, and drink preferences.

---

## 6. Communicating with Guests (Email Dispatcher)

The booking detail view includes ready-to-dispatch email templates:

### Available Templates:
- **Pre-Arrival Invitation:** Welcomes the guest, provides their assigned suite details, and includes their secure 4-digit PIN to access their online pre-arrival form.
- **PIN Reminder:** A concise reminder sent 7 days and 3 days before arrival if responses are still pending.

### How to Dispatch:
1. Select the email template tab (**Invitation** or **Reminder**).
2. Review the live preview with guest names and suite PINs automatically populated.
3. Click **✉️ Send via Default Email App** or **📋 Copy Email Body** to paste into your official email client.

## 7. Managing Suites & Rooms

Kishindo Canyon Lodge features 4 distinct luxury sanctuary suites:

- **Suite 1: Tibo** (Max 2 Guests) — Panoramic wrap-around sun deck, outdoor daybed, and private lounge.
- **Suite 2: Ussuri** (Max 3 Guests) — Canyon overhang balcony, outdoor & indoor showers, interleading door to Zaria.
- **Suite 3: Zaria** (Max 2 Guests) — Secluded Karoo rockery overhang, outdoor starlit stone shower.
- **Suite 4: Guide's Room** (Max 2 Guests) — Dedicated sanctuary suite for private photographic tour guides.

> [!NOTE]
> Photographic tour guides stay in **Suite 4 (Guide's Room)** as guests with their own 4-digit Suite PIN. They do not log into the lodge operations portal.

---

## 8. Role Differences: Anchor vs. Admin
As a Lodge Anchor, you have access to all day-to-day hospitality and reservation tools. However, for governance reasons:
- **Cancelling Bookings (`🚫 Cancel Booking`):** Anchors can cancel reservations when notified of cancellations.
- **Permanent Deletion (`🗑️ Delete Booking Permanently`):** Restricted to `Admin Role`. If a duplicate or test record must be completely expunged from the database, request an Admin to delete it.
- **Team User Management (`👥 Manage Team`):** Restricted to `Admin Role`.
